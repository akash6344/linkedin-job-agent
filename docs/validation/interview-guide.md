# Problem validation interview guide

Use this script with **15 India-based early-career technical candidates** (0–3 years, fresher to mid-junior). Goal: validate willingness to pay, job-source mix, email OAuth comfort, and human-in-the-loop apply flow before charging.

## Recruiting criteria

- Based in India (any city / remote)
- Actively job searching or planning to within 60 days
- Target roles: Software / Backend / Full-stack / Python / AI / GenAI
- Mix: 8 freshers, 7 with 1–3 years experience

## Script (25–30 minutes)

### Warm-up (3 min)

1. What roles are you applying for right now?
2. How many applications do you send in a typical week?
3. Which platforms do you use most? (Naukri, LinkedIn, company careers, Telegram groups, referrals, other)

### Pain (8 min)

4. What takes the most time: finding roles, writing emails/cover letters, filling forms, or tracking replies?
5. Walk me through your last application end-to-end.
6. What makes you skip a job post?
7. Have you used any autofill / auto-apply tools? What worked / failed?

### Product reaction (8 min)

Describe the product in one sentence:

> “A job-search copilot that finds relevant openings for your profile, drafts a tailored email or form checklist, and asks you to approve before anything is sent. You connect Gmail with Google sign-in (no app password). Applications stay in a tracker.”

8. On a 1–10 scale, how useful does that sound?
9. Would you connect Gmail via Google OAuth so the product can send from your address after you click Approve?
10. Would you prefer: (a) review every draft, (b) auto-send only when fit score is high and daily cap is low, (c) fully autonomous?
11. Which job sources must work on day one for you to pay?

### Pricing (5 min)

Show three options (ex-GST):

| Pass | Price | Cap |
|------|-------|-----|
| Free | ₹0 | 10 matches/week, 3 drafts/month |
| Job Search Pass | ₹299 / 30 days | 30 approved sends |
| Pro / 90-day | ₹699 / 30d or ₹1,499 / 90d | 100 sends / month equivalent |

12. Which would you buy today, if any? Why?
13. What would make ₹299 feel expensive? What would make ₹1,499 feel cheap?
14. Prefer one-time pass or monthly auto-renew?

### Close (2 min)

15. Would you join a 30-day private beta for free in exchange for weekly feedback?
16. Who else should we interview?

## Pass criteria before building payments

- ≥10 / 15 interviews complete
- ≥60% rate usefulness ≥7
- ≥50% comfortable with OAuth send-after-approve
- Clear top-3 source list that does **not** require selling LinkedIn scraping as the core product
- At least 5 candidates willing to join beta
