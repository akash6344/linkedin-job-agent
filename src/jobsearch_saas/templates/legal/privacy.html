Privacy Policy

Aligned with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 principles. Notice version 2026-08-v1.

What we collect

  • Account data: name, email, phone, links you provide
  • Resumes and parsed skills
  • Search preferences and application history
  • OAuth tokens for Gmail (encrypted) when you connect send/read
  • Payment metadata from Razorpay (we do not store full card numbers)

Purposes (separate consents)

  • Job matching — find and score openings for you
  • Email sending — send approved applications from your Gmail
  • Product communications — receipts, beta surveys, product tips

Your rights

You can view consent history, export your data, revoke email access, and delete your account from Settings. Withdrawal of matching consent ends processing for that purpose.

Retention

We keep active account data while your account exists. After deletion, personal content is erased; limited payment stubs may remain for tax/compliance.
